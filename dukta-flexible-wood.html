Dukta Flexible Wood

About The Project

This project is an experiential expo stand designed for Dukta, a brand known for its innovative flexible wood technology. The circular form symbolizes continuity and transformation, expressing the adaptable and dynamic nature of the material.

Soft curves, layered geometry, and a visitor-focused flow invite users into a tactile and sensory experience, showcasing the craftsmanship and warmth of wood. Carefully selected materials and lighting enhance visual narrative — turning a trade fair booth into a meaningful design statement about movement, texture, and spatial harmony.
