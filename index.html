İRİS AKAL

Interior Architect

Born in 2004 in Ankara, I graduated from TED University in Interior Architecture and Environmental Design. A former ballerina and multi-instrumentalist, I bring an artistic lens to spatial design.

I'm passionate about interior living design and environmental psychology—exploring how spaces shape our stories and emotions. Through adaptive reuse and sustainable design, I create environments that breathe, connect and invite experience.

For me, architecture is memory, connection and the roots that tie people to place. I dream boldly, work passionately and transform ideas into spaces worth remembering.
