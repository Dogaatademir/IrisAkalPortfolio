Transfluence

About The Project

Transfluence reimagines the historic hangars as a living ecological and cultural system powered by aquaponics. Water cycles through productive green layers that nurture plant growth while shaping educational, social, and exhibition spaces.

The design emphasizes transparency, spatial rhythm, and seamless interaction between people and nature — connecting architecture with regenerative environmental processes.

By merging agriculture, research, gastronomy, and public engagement, Transfluence transforms the site into a sustainable landscape of renewal, where growth, knowledge, and community flow continuously together.
