Iris Akal

I’m Iris Akal, born in 2004 in Ankara, where I still live and create. I graduated from TED University in Interior Architecture and Environmental Design, discovering how deeply spaces can shape our stories.

I’m passionate about interior living design and environmental psychology—exploring how the spaces we inhabit influence who we are and how we feel. Before pursuing architecture, I was a professional ballerina and I play multiple musical instruments. Art has always been the language I think and create with, and now I transform that artistic background into spatial design.

For me, architecture is not just structure but memory, connection, and the roots that tie people to place. I focus on adaptive reuse and sustainable design that supports communities and nature—like a seed finding its light. Through experimentation with visuals, materials, sound, and movement, I aim to create environments that breathe and invite people to experience, not just observe.

I dream boldly, work passionately, and cherish the moment when an idea becomes a space someone will remember.
